Sri Lankan cuisine is known for its bold flavors and rich spices. One of the most famous spicy dishes is Kottu Roti, a popular street food made from shredded godamba roti, vegetables, eggs, and a choice of meat (chicken, beef, or mutton), all mixed with an array of spices like roasted curry powder, chili powder, and black pepper. It is usually served with a side of spicy curry gravy.

Another classic is the Sri Lankan Chicken Curry, often made with a base of coconut milk but heavily spiced with cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and fiery red chili powder. The 'black curry' variant uses deeply roasted spices to give it an intense flavor and dark color.

Pol Sambol is a very common spicy condiment, made from freshly grated coconut, red onions, dried red chilies, lime juice, and Maldive fish. It is very spicy and is typically eaten with rice, string hoppers, or bread.

Lunumiris is another fiery paste made entirely of crushed red chilies, onions, salt, and lime juice. It is extremely spicy and is an essential accompaniment to milk rice (Kiribath) and hoppers (Aappa).
