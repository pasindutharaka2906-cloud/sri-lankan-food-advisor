Sri Lankan sweets are often made with jaggery (palm sugar), coconut milk, and rice flour. 

Watalappan is a classic Malay-influenced Sri Lankan dessert. It is a steamed custard made with coconut milk, kithul jaggery, eggs, cashew nuts, and spices like cardamom and nutmeg. It has a very rich, sweet, and caramel-like flavor, and is a staple at Muslim festivals and special occasions.

Kavum (Oil Cake) is a traditional sweet made from rice flour and kithul treacle, deep-fried in oil. It has a distinct shape with a top knot and is a must-have during the Sinhala and Tamil New Year.

Kokis is a crispy, deep-fried sweet made from rice flour and coconut milk. While less sweet than Kavum, it is a very popular festive snack.

Milk Toffee (Kiri Toffee) is a popular confection made with sweetened condensed milk, sugar, butter, and sometimes flavored with vanilla and studded with cashew nuts. It is very sweet and often served with tea.
